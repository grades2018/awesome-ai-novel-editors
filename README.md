## Manuscript Rewrite & Generative Editors

Tools that rewrite, expand, or voice-shift novel prose while you stay in control of the draft.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite for polishing, restructuring, expanding, or voice-shifting existing drafts (Word/Markdown/text/HTML) while preserving what already works; also plans/drafts full manuscripts in the author’s voice.
- ⭐ [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/) - AI fiction suite with rewrite, expand, and describe tools popular with novelists during revision.
- [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Novel workspace with AI assist grounded in your Codex for context-aware scene and chapter edits.
- [Lex](https://lex.page/) - Distraction-free AI writing editor useful for scene rewrites and revision passes.
- [ShortlyAI](https://www.shortlyai.com/) - Long-form AI writing assistant for continuing and tightening prose.
- [EditGPT](https://editgpt.app/) - AI document editor for tracked suggestions and rewrite passes on manuscripts.
- [Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com/) - AI rewriting assistant for sentence-level clarity and tone variants.
- [Fictionate.Me](https://www.fictionate.me/) - Fiction-oriented AI writing tool for scene and chapter generation during revision.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI drafting and rewrite helpers for long manuscripts.

## Style & Line Editing

Report-driven and grammar tools for sentence craft, repetition, and clarity.

- ⭐ [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - In-depth style and manuscript reports (pacing, dialogue tags, overused words, and more) favored by fiction authors.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Grammar and clarity checking for drafts before beta or editor handoff.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://www.hemingwayapp.com/) - Readability-focused editor that flags dense sentences and passive constructions.
- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Fiction-focused style analysis with pacing and dialogue checks against genre patterns.

## Developmental Critique & Analysis

Analytical tools that read the whole manuscript for plot, pacing, and character, usually without rewriting your words.

- ⭐ [Marlowe (Authors.ai)](https://authors.ai/marlowe/) - Analytical AI for fiction: developmental feedback on plot, character, pacing, and theme vs genre comps (analysis, not generation).
- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Reader/editor screen for line-level review while Manuscript Rewrite handles larger structural and voice passes.
- [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) - Long-context AI assistant strong at chapter- and manuscript-level critique when prompted carefully.

## Fiction Benchmarking

Tools that compare your manuscript patterns to published fiction in your genre.

- ⭐ [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Genre-aware benchmarking of pacing, word choice, and other patterns against published fiction.
- [Marlowe (Authors.ai)](https://authors.ai/marlowe/) - Genre-aware manuscript analysis comparing structure and comps for fiction authors.

## Story Structure Editors

Structural editing aids for scenes, story beats, and narrative logic.

- [Fictionary](https://www.fictionary.co/) - Story editing software for scene-by-scene structure, character arcs, and plot evaluation.
- [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) - Visual timeline and beat templates useful when restructuring a novel mid-edit.
- [One Stop for Writers](https://onestopforwriters.com/) - Character and story-map resources helpful during developmental passes.

## Formatting & Export After Edit

Get a clean file out after the edit pass, EPUB, Word, Markdown, or print interiors.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Formatting and line edits via the reader/editor, with export to EPUB, Word, and Markdown.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Cross-platform book formatter for ebook and print interiors after the edit is locked.
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) - Mac book formatting software popular once the manuscript is editorially complete.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio plus access to professional editors.
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) - Open-source converter for moving an edited manuscript between Markdown, Word, and EPUB.

## General AI Assistants for Revision

General-purpose models novelists use for chapter notes, sensitivity passes, and rewrite brainstorming.

- [ChatGPT](https://openai.com/chatgpt/overview) - General AI chatbot for revision notes, dialogue variants, and blurb polish.
- [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) - Multimodal assistant for research checks and drafting support during edits.
- [LivingWriter](https://www.livingwriter.com/) - Manuscript editor with AI helpers useful alongside a dedicated critique tool.
