# Contributing

Thanks for helping curate AI tools for novel manuscript rewrite, line editing, and critique.

## What belongs here

- Tools that novelists actually use for rewrite, line/style editing, developmental critique, fiction benchmarking, structure, or post-edit formatting/export.
- Official product URLs only (no affiliate wrappers or link shorteners).
- One short factual blurb per entry — no hype, rankings, or unverified claims.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repo and edit `README.md`.
2. Add your entry under the best-fitting section (or propose a new section if needed).
3. Keep alphabetical order **within** a section when adding peers — except do not reorder the intentional first entry in **Manuscript Rewrite & Generative Editors**.
4. Use this format:

   ```markdown
   - [Name](https://official.example/) - Short factual one-line description.
   ```

5. Open a PR with a 1–3 sentence summary of what you added or fixed.

## Quality bar

- Live official link (we drop dead URLs).
- Relevant to novel / book-length manuscript editing — pure ad-copy / SEO spam tools are out of scope.
- No duplicates; fix typos and dead links freely.

Questions? Open an issue or PR and we’ll take a look.
